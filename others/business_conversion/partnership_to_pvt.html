OVERVIEW

Preserving Business Legacy while Transitioning to Corporate Structure

Partnership firms inherently suffer from significant structural limitations: unlimited personal liability for partners, inability to raise equity capital from institutional investors, lack of perpetual succession, and difficulty in transferring business shares seamlessly.

Under Chapter XXI (Section 366 to 374) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 3 of the Companies (Authorized to Register) Rules, 2014, both registered and unregistered partnership firms having 2 or more partners can directly convert into a Private Limited Company. This statutory route allows the business to transition without incurring costly stamp duty on asset re-registrations or triggering capital gains tax under Section 47(xiii) of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

KEY ADVANTAGES

Why Convert Your Partnership Firm to Pvt Ltd?

Unlock corporate scale, protect personal assets, and make your business venture-ready.

01

Limited Liability Protection

In a partnership firm, partners have joint and unlimited liability where personal assets can be attached. A Private Limited structure insulates personal property, limiting liability to unpaid share capital.

02

Equity Capital & VC Funding

Partnerships cannot issue shares or offer equity stakes. Converting to a Pvt Ltd allows the entity to issue preferential shares, equity shares, or convertible instruments to Angel and VC investors.

03

Section 47(xiii) Tax Immunity

The conversion is tax-neutral and exempt from Capital Gains tax provided all assets/liabilities transfer and partners retain 50%+ shareholding with voting rights for at least 5 years.

04

Perpetual Succession & Brand Value

Partnership firms dissolve on the death or retirement of a partner. A Pvt Ltd company exists independently, ensuring unbroken legacy, continuous operational contracts, and brand goodwill.

05

ESOPs for Employee Retention

Attract and retain top executive talent by structuring Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs), a growth mechanism unavailable under standard partnership models.

06

GST & Credit Rating Enhancement

Transfer accumulated Input Tax Credit (ITC) via GST procedures. Corporate structures enjoy far better credit ratings and reduced borrowing interest rates from scheduled banks.

INCOME TAX ACT, 1961

Section 47(xiii) Tax Exemption Conditions

Ensure 100% tax neutrality and prevent capital gains clawbacks by satisfying these mandatory statutory criteria.

1. Total Business Vesting

All assets and liabilities of the firm immediately before conversion must become the assets and liabilities of the new company. Selective asset transfer is strictly prohibited.

2. Complete Share Allotment

All partners of the firm must become shareholders in the company in the same proportion as their capital accounts stood on the date of conversion.

3. Partner Voting Power Retention

The aggregate shareholding of former partners must not fall below 50% of the total voting power for a continuous period of 5 years from the date of conversion.

4. Zero Cash Consideration

Partners must receive consideration exclusively in the form of share allotment in the company. No cash or secondary consideration can be paid to partners.

DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST

Comprehensive Document Checklist

1. Firm & Statutory Documents

Original Partnership Deed, ROF Registration Certificate (if registered), supplementary deeds, up-to-date Income Tax Returns, and firm PAN card.

2. Partner / Director KYC Documents

PAN card, Aadhaar, Passport (mandatory for foreign nationals), Voter ID/Driving Licence, and utility bills/bank statements not older than 2 months for each partner.

3. Registered Office Proofs

Registered Lease/Rent Agreement or Sale Deed, recent Utility Bill (electricity, gas, water within 60 days), and signed NOC from property owner.

4. Accounting & Legal Certificates

CA-certified Statement of Assets and Liabilities (within 30 days), Certified List of Partners & Shareholding, Creditor NOCs, and Newspaper Copies (Form URC-2).

CONVERSION WORKFLOW

10-Step Operational Execution Plan

1. DSC & DIN Allotment

Obtain Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) and Director Identification Numbers (DIN) for all partners stepping into directorship roles.

2. Partners Meeting & Resolution

Convene formal meeting of partners to pass unanimous/3/4th majority resolution authorizing conversion, approving share proportions, and nominating directors.

3. Name Reservation via RUN / SPICe+ Part A

Reserve proposed company name on MCA portal indicating "Conversion of Partnership Firm" as the main object.

4. Public Notice Advertisement (Form URC-2)

Publish advertisement in Form URC-2 in 1 English and 1 Vernacular newspaper circulating in the district of firm's place of business to seek objections (21-day notice window).

5. Statement of Accounts & Creditor NOCs

Obtain audited financial statements certified by a practicing CA (within 30 days) and secure written consent/NOC from all secured creditors.

6. Filing Form URC-1 & SPICe+ Part B

Submit e-Form URC-1 along with SPICe+ incorporation forms, e-MOA (INC-33), e-AOA (INC-34), and AGILE-PRO-S to the Registrar of Companies (ROC).

7. Verification & COI Issuance

ROC verifies documents and issues Certificate of Incorporation (COI) containing Company Identification Number (CIN), PAN, and TAN.

8. Bank Account & Capital Transfer

Open corporate current account, update bank mandate, and transfer capital balances from partnership accounts to corporate shares.

9. GST & License Intimation

Update GST portal by transferring Input Tax Credit using Form GST ITC-02 and intimate municipal, trade, and regulatory authorities of structure change.

10. INC-20A Commencement of Business

File Form INC-20A within 180 days of incorporation to declare share capital subscription deposit and commence corporate operations legally.
