WHY OUTSOURCE ACCOUNTING?

Accurate Books. On-Time Filings. Investor-Ready Numbers.

Companies must maintain proper books under Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013 (minimum 8-year retention). GST and TDS add monthly and quarterly deadlines. Outsourced accounting keeps records compliant, reduces in-house cost and frees founders to focus on growth.

01

Section 128 Compliance

Proper books of account; penalty of ₹25,000 can apply for default under the Act.

02

Tax Deadline Cover

GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, TDS returns and ITR handled so late fees and interest are minimised.

03

Clear Financial View

Monthly P&L, balance sheet and cash flow for decisions and stakeholders.

04

Cost Efficiency

Professional support at a fraction of a full-time finance team cost.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting? +

Bookkeeping is the systematic recording of daily transactions and bank reconciliation. Accounting analyses and reports that data — financial statements, tax computation and compliance outputs under the Companies Act and tax laws.

Who needs outsourced accounting? +

Registered companies, LLPs, startups and GST-registered businesses that need proper books under Section 128, timely GST/TDS filings and clear financial reports without a full in-house team.

What software do you support? +

Common cloud and desktop tools such as Zoho Books, Tally and QuickBooks. We can work with your existing setup or help set up a new one during onboarding.

How long does onboarding take? +

Typically 2–5 working days once access, opening balances and document handoff are complete.

Is GST filing included? +

Starter and higher packages can include GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B (and annual return support where scoped). Exact inclusions depend on the package you choose.

What does the monthly fee cover? +

Professional work for the agreed scope (bookkeeping, reconciliations, returns, statements, reports). Government or portal fees, if any, are separate at actuals. Volume and complexity can affect pricing.
