FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is company registration? +

Company registration is the legal process of incorporating a business entity with the applicable government authority. The exact process depends on the type of entity being formed.

Which company structure is best for me? +

It depends on factors such as the number of founders, liability preferences, funding plans, ownership and compliance requirements. Our experts can help you compare suitable structures.

Can I register a company from my home? +

A residential address may be used as a registered office where applicable, subject to the required documentation and legal requirements.

What documents are required? +

Typical requirements can include identity proof, address proof, photographs and registered-office documents. Exact requirements depend on the entity and applicant.

How long does registration take? +

Processing time depends on the entity, documentation, government verification and whether additional clarification is required.

What happens after incorporation? +

Depending on the entity and activities, you may need to complete post-incorporation filings, tax registrations, banking and ongoing compliance requirements.
