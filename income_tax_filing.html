FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who must file an ITR? +

Any person whose gross total income exceeds the basic exemption limit for the relevant year. Filing may also be required in other cases (e.g. certain high-value transactions or foreign assets) as per current law.

What is the difference between old and new tax regime? +

The old regime allows many deductions and exemptions (e.g. 80C, 80D, HRA). The new regime offers lower slab rates but disallows most of those deductions. We compare both for your income profile.

Which ITR form should I use? +

ITR-1 (Sahaj) for simple salaried cases; ITR-2 for capital gains or multiple properties; ITR-3 for business/profession; ITR-4 (Sugam) for presumptive taxation; ITR-5/6/7 for firms, companies and trusts. We select the correct form for you.

What documents are required? +

PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16/16A, Form 26AS, AIS, bank details, and proofs of income and deductions. Business and audit cases need books and tax audit report where applicable.

What is the due date for filing? +

Typically 31 July for individuals and non-audit cases; later dates apply for tax audit and transfer pricing cases. Always confirm the current year’s notified due dates.

What happens if I file late? +

Late filing can attract a fee under Section 234F, interest under Sections 234A/234B/234C, and in serious cases further consequences under the Act.
