OVERVIEW

What is a Trade License?

A trade license is a mandatory permit issued by the local municipal corporation that authorises a business to carry on a specific trade or commercial activity at a particular premises within municipal limits. Without a valid trade license, operating a shop, office, restaurant, factory or warehouse can attract fines, sealing of premises or prosecution under municipal laws.

Requirements, fees and portals differ across 4,000+ municipal bodies in India. Major issuers include MCD (Delhi), BMC (Mumbai), BBMP (Bengaluru), GHMC (Hyderabad), GCC (Chennai) and KMC (Kolkata). Online application is available in most metro and many tier-2 cities. Processing typically takes 7–30 working days after document verification and any required inspection.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Identity Proof

PAN and Aadhaar of proprietor, partner or authorised director; passport-size photograph.

2. Premises Address Proof

Electricity bill, property tax receipt or water bill (recent) in the name of owner or tenant.

3. Ownership / Tenancy

Sale deed or property documents (owned); rent agreement / lease deed + NOC from landlord (rented).

4. Business Documents

Certificate of Incorporation / Partnership Deed / GST certificate if registered; layout plan of premises.

5. Additional NOCs (if required)

Fire NOC, pollution clearance or health department NOC for factories, restaurants and certain trades.

6. Application Form

Filled municipal application with trade category, area, employee count and commencement details.

PROCESS

How Trade License Registration Works

1. Document Preparation

Collect identity, premises, ownership/tenancy and business documents; identify correct municipal body and trade category.

2. Portal Registration

Register on the municipal corporation portal (e.g. MCD, BMC, BBMP) and create the application.

3. Form & Upload

Fill business and premises details; upload scanned documents as per portal specifications.

4. Fee Payment

Pay the applicable trade license fee online. Fee depends on category, area and city.

5. Inspection & Certificate

Municipal inspection (if required) of premises; on clearance, trade license certificate is issued.
