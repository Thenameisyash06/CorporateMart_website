OVERVIEW

What is MSME Udyam Registration?

MSME Udyam Registration is the official government recognition for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises in India. It replaced the earlier Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM) system from July 2020 and is the only valid form of MSME registration recognised by the Government of India.

Registration is done on the Udyam portal (udyamregistration.gov.in) maintained by the Ministry of MSME. It is free, fully online and based on self-declaration. Details are validated through Aadhaar, PAN and GST integrations. On success, you receive a permanent Udyam Registration Number and an e-certificate with QR code — lifetime validity, no renewal required.

CLASSIFICATION

Micro · Small · Medium

Category Investment (Plant & Machinery / Equipment) Annual Turnover
Micro Up to ₹1 crore Up to ₹5 crore
Small Up to ₹10 crore Up to ₹50 crore
Medium Up to ₹50 crore Up to ₹250 crore
Both investment and turnover criteria must be met. Crossing either limit moves the enterprise to the higher category. Export turnover is excluded from turnover calculation. Same limits apply to manufacturing and services.
BENEFITS

Why Register as MSME?

01

Priority Sector Lending

Banks allocate lending to MSMEs at preferential rates — typically 1–2% lower than standard commercial rates.

02

Collateral-Free Loans

CGTMSE scheme supports loans (up to specified limits) without collateral or third-party guarantee.

03

Tender Preference

Purchase preference in government procurement (e.g. 25% for MSEs); exemption from earnest money deposit in many cases.

04

Delayed Payment Protection

Buyers must pay MSMEs within 45 days under the MSMED Act; interest claimable via MSME Samadhan.

05

Subsidies & Schemes

Access to capital subsidy (e.g. CLCSS), patent fee concessions, electricity concessions and other MSME schemes.

06

Official Recognition

Udyam certificate accepted by banks, departments and institutions nationwide for loans, tenders and compliance.

ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Register?

01

Within Limits

Enterprises with investment and turnover within the Micro, Small or Medium thresholds.

02

All Organisation Types

Proprietorship, partnership, LLP, company, HUF, cooperative, society and similar entities.

03

Manufacturing & Services

Same classification criteria apply to both manufacturing and service enterprises.

04

Existing or New

New enterprises and those migrating from old UAM can register or update on the Udyam portal.

PROCESS

How Udyam Registration Works

1. Classification Check

Assess investment and turnover to confirm Micro / Small / Medium category.

2. Aadhaar & PAN Ready

Aadhaar of proprietor / managing partner / authorised signatory linked to mobile; enterprise PAN.

3. Portal Filing

Register on udyamregistration.gov.in; fill enterprise details; OTP verification via Aadhaar.

4. Validation

System validates PAN, GST (if any) and other details through government databases.

5. Certificate

Udyam Registration Number and e-certificate with QR code generated — download and use immediately.

DOCUMENTS

What You Need (No Uploads)

1. Aadhaar

Of proprietor, managing partner or authorised signatory — linked to active mobile for OTP.

2. PAN

Enterprise PAN (for company/LLP/partnership) or individual PAN (for proprietorship).

3. GSTIN (if any)

Recommended for auto-validation when the business is GST-registered.

4. Bank Details

Account number and IFSC for the enterprise bank account.

5. Investment & Turnover

Plant & machinery/equipment investment and annual turnover figures for classification.

6. NIC Codes

Business activity codes (NIC) describing the nature of the enterprise.
