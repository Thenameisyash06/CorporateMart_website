OVERVIEW

What is the Labour Welfare Fund?

The Labour Welfare Fund (LWF) is a statutory contribution scheme set up by state governments to promote the welfare of workers. It is administered by the state Labour Welfare Board under the respective State Labour Welfare Fund Act. Employers and employees contribute small fixed amounts that fund housing assistance, education scholarships, medical aid and recreational facilities for workers and their families.

Unlike PF and ESI (central, percentage-based), LWF is state-specific with nominal fixed rates. Despite low amounts, non-compliance can attract penalties, interest and prosecution. Employers in LWF states must register, calculate contributions correctly and file returns on time. Multi-state employers must comply with each state’s rates and due dates.

WHO NEEDS IT

Who Must Register & Contribute

01

Employers in LWF States

All employers with employees in states that have an active Labour Welfare Fund Act must register and contribute.

02

Establishments of All Sizes

Thresholds (if any) vary by state. Many states apply LWF once employees are engaged, regardless of headcount.

03

Multi-State Employers

If you operate in more than one LWF state, you must register and file in each applicable state.

04

Not a Substitute for PF/ESI

LWF is separate from EPF and ESI. You may need all three depending on coverage and thresholds.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Entity Proof

Certificate of Incorporation, LLP agreement, partnership deed or proprietorship proof.

2. PAN

PAN of the establishment (company, firm, LLP or proprietor).

3. Address Proof

Rent agreement, utility bill or property documents for the establishment.

4. Employee Details

Employee register / muster roll with headcount for contribution calculation.

5. Bank Details

Cancelled cheque or bank statement for the establishment account.

6. Other Licenses

Shop & Establishment, Factory License or GST certificate if available.

PROCESS

How LWF Registration Works

1. State Applicability

Identify states where you operate and confirm LWF Act is in force for those locations.

2. Document Prep

Gather entity proof, PAN, address, employee register and bank details.

3. Portal Registration

Register on the state Labour Welfare Board portal and submit the application.

4. Certificate

Obtain LWF registration / account number after verification by the Board.

5. Contribute & File

Calculate contributions, deposit on time and file half-yearly or annual returns as per state rules.
