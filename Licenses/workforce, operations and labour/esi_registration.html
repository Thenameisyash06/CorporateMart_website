OVERVIEW

What is ESI Registration?

ESI Registration is mandatory compliance under the Employees' State Insurance Act, 1948. Establishments employing 10 or more employees (in most notified areas) with wages up to ₹21,000 per month must register with the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC).

ESI is a self-financing social security and health insurance scheme providing medical care, sickness benefits, maternity benefits, disablement and dependants’ benefits. On registration you receive a 17-digit Employer Code; each covered employee gets an Insurance Number (IP). Contributions (employer 3.25% + employee 0.75% of gross wages) and returns are due by the 15th of the following month.

Law ESI Act, 1948
Authority ESIC (esic.gov.in)

Wage Limit ₹21,000 / month (gross)

WHO MUST REGISTER

Eligibility & Coverage

01

10+ Employees

Mandatory in most notified areas when the establishment employs 10 or more persons. Some states (e.g. Maharashtra) use 20.

02

Wage Ceiling

Employees with gross wages up to ₹21,000 per month are covered; ₹25,000 for persons with disability.

03

Once Covered, Always Covered

Once the establishment is covered, ESI continues even if headcount later falls below the threshold.

04

Notified Areas

Scheme applies in areas notified by ESIC — covering most urban and semi-urban locations.

CONTRIBUTIONS

Contribution Structure

Component Rate Paid By
Employer Contribution 3.25% of gross wages Employer
Employee Contribution 0.75% of gross wages Employee (deducted)
Total 4.00% of gross wages —
Contributions are calculated on gross wages (not basic only). Employees earning less than ₹137 per day may be exempt from the employee share. Due by the 15th of the following month.
BENEFITS

What ESI Provides

01

Medical Care

Cashless treatment at ESIC hospitals, dispensaries and empaneled private hospitals for insured person and family.

02

Sickness Benefit

Cash compensation at about 70% of wages during certified sickness (up to 91 days in a benefit period).

03

Maternity Benefit

26 weeks paid leave at full wages for eligible female employees.

04

Disablement & Dependants

Compensation for employment injury and dependants’ benefit in case of death due to employment injury.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Entity PAN

PAN of the establishment (company, LLP, firm or proprietor).

2. Incorporation Proof

Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed or shop/factory registration.

3. Address Proof

Rent agreement, utility bill or property documents of the establishment.

4. Bank Details

Cancelled cheque or bank statement of the business account.

5. Employee List

Names, Aadhaar, date of joining, wages and family details for covered employees.

6. Directors / Partners

List of directors or partners with identification and authorisation if required.

PROCESS

How ESI Registration Works

1. Eligibility Check

Confirm headcount threshold (10+ / 20 as applicable) and wage coverage in your notified area.

2. Document Prep

Gather PAN, incorporation proof, address, bank details and employee list.

3. ESIC Portal Filing

Register and submit the employer registration application on the ESIC portal.

4. Employer Code

Receive 17-digit Employer Code after verification — typically 5–15 working days.

5. IP Numbers & Comply

Generate Insurance Numbers (IP) for employees and start monthly contributions by the 15th.
