OVERVIEW

What is ISO Certification?

ISO certification is third-party confirmation that your management system meets an ISO standard. ISO itself develops standards; it does not audit or issue certificates. An independent certification body (CB), after a two-stage audit, issues the certificate. The certificate is only as credible as the CB’s accreditation (e.g. NABCB under QCI in India, or another IAF MLA signatory).

Certification is voluntary in law and often compulsory in commerce — tenders, OEMs and enterprise buyers demand it. Validity is typically 3 years with annual surveillance. We support gap analysis, documentation, internal audit and management review preparation, and coordination with accredited CBs — not the sale of unaccredited “certificates” that fail the first buyer check.

PROCESS

How ISO Certification Works

1. Standard & Scope

Select the right standard and draft a realistic scope (sites, products, processes).

2. Gap Analysis

Clause-by-clause gap register against the standard before you commit to timeline or audit fee.

3. Documentation & Implementation

Documented information, risk register, internal audit and management review support.

4. Stage 1 & Stage 2

Coordination with the accredited CB; Stage 1 (docs) then Stage 2 (implementation) audits.

5. Certificate & Surveillance

Certificate issued for 3 years; annual surveillance audits; nonconformity closure support.
