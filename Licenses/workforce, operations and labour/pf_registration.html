OVERVIEW

What is PF / EPF Registration?

PF Registration is mandatory compliance under the Employees' Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952. Establishments with 20 or more employees must register with the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) and contribute to the Employee Provident Fund.

EPF is a social security scheme providing retirement savings, pension (EPS) and life insurance (EDLI). Both employer and employee contribute 12% of basic wages + DA. The employer’s 12% is split into EPF (3.67%), EPS (8.33%, capped) and related charges. On registration you receive an Establishment Code used for all PF transactions. Monthly ECR filing and payment are due by the 15th of the next month.

Law EPF & MP Act, 1952
Authority EPFO (Unified Portal)
Threshold 20 or more employees
WHO MUST REGISTER

Eligibility & Coverage

01

Mandatory (20+)

Factories, shops and establishments with 20 or more employees on any day must register within one month of crossing the threshold.

02

Voluntary

Establishments with fewer than 20 employees can register voluntarily with consent of the majority of employees.

03

Once Covered, Always Covered

Once covered under EPF, coverage continues even if headcount later falls below 20.

04

Employee Coverage

Employees with basic + DA up to ₹15,000 are mandatorily covered; higher-paid can opt in with employer consent.

CONTRIBUTIONS

Contribution Structure (Indicative)

Component Employee Employer
EPF (Provident Fund) 12% of Basic + DA 3.67% of Basic + DA
EPS (Pension) — 8.33% (max on ₹15,000)
EDLI (Insurance) — 0.50% of Basic + DA
Admin Charges — ~0.50% (EPF admin; min applies)
Total (approx) 12% ~13%
Rates and caps are subject to EPFO notifications. Confirm current rates on the EPFO portal. Payment and ECR due by the 15th of the following month.
BENEFITS

Why PF Registration Matters

01

Legal Compliance

Avoid penalties and prosecution under the EPF Act for non-registration or non-payment.

02

Employee Welfare

Retirement corpus, pension (EPS) and life cover (EDLI) for the workforce.

03

Tax Benefits

Employer contribution is a business expense; employee contribution qualifies for 80C.

04

Tenders & Credibility

Valid PF registration is often required for government contracts and builds employer brand.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Entity PAN

PAN of the establishment (company, LLP, firm or proprietor).

2. Incorporation Proof

Certificate of Incorporation, LLP agreement or Partnership Deed.

3. Address Proof

Rent agreement with utility bill or property documents of the establishment.

4. Bank Details

Cancelled cheque or bank statement of the business account.

5. DSC

Digital Signature Certificate of the authorised signatory for portal filing.

6. Employee List

List of employees with Aadhaar, joining date, basic wages and bank details.

PROCESS

How PF Registration Works

1. Eligibility Check

Confirm headcount (20+) or voluntary registration and gather entity and employee details.

2. Document Prep

PAN, incorporation proof, address, bank, DSC and employee list ready in portal-accepted format.

3. EPFO Portal Filing

Register on the EPFO Unified Portal and submit the establishment registration application with DSC.

4. Establishment Code

Receive PF Establishment Code after verification — typically 5–7 working days.

5. Enrol & Comply

Enrol employees (UAN), start monthly contributions and file ECR by the 15th of each following month.
