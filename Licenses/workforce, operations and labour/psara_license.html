OVERVIEW

What is a PSARA License?

A PSARA License is an official permit under the Private Security Agencies (Regulation) Act, 2005 that authorises a person or entity to operate a private security agency in India. The Act regulates the private security industry so that guards are trained, verified and supervised to prescribed standards.

Under Section 4, no one may carry on the business of a private security agency without a license from the state Controlling Authority (typically DGP or Commissioner of Police). The application is filed in Form P under the PSARA Rules 2006. The license is state-specific and valid for 5 years. Multi-state operations require a separate license in each state. Non-compliance can attract imprisonment up to 1 year and/or fine up to ₹25,000 under Section 14.

LICENSE TYPES

Fresh · Renewal · Additional State

01

Fresh License

New agencies starting operations for the first time in a state. Full documentation, police verification and premises inspection.

02

Renewal

Existing licensees applying at least 60 days before the 5-year expiry with updated documents and compliance record.

03

Additional State

Agencies already licensed in one state expanding to another — fresh application to the new state’s Controlling Authority.

04

Multi-State

Coordinated filing across multiple states for pan-India operations. Each state issues its own license.

ELIGIBILITY

Key Requirements

Indian Entity / Citizen

02

Police Verification

Background checks on directors, partners, principal officer and deployed security personnel.

03

Training Infrastructure

Own training facility or tie-up with an approved training centre for 100–160 hours of guard training.

04

Premises & Net Worth

Suitable office/premises and net worth certificate as prescribed by the state.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Entity Registration

Certificate of Incorporation, LLP Agreement, Partnership Deed or proprietorship proof.

2. Identity of Key Persons

PAN and Aadhaar of all directors/partners and principal officer.

3. Premises Proof

Rent agreement or ownership deed and utility bill of the registered office.

4. Police Verification

Character / police verification reports for applicant and key personnel.

5. Training Details

MOU with approved training centre or documentation of own training facility.

6. Net Worth & Form P

Net worth certificate and duly filled Form P as under PSARA Rules 2006.

PROCESS

How PSARA License Registration Works

1. Eligibility & State Check

Confirm applicability and identify the Controlling Authority for the state of operation.

2. Document Preparation

Entity papers, identity proofs, premises, training tie-up, net worth and Form P.

3. Application Filing

Submit Form P and supporting documents to the Controlling Authority with prescribed fee.

4. Verification

Police verification of key persons and premises inspection as required by the state.

5. License Grant

License issued for 5 years after approval. Typical timeline 60–90 days. Renew 60 days before expiry.
