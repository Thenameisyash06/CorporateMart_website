OVERVIEW

What is NGO DARPAN Registration?

NGO Darpan is an online platform of NITI Aayog that creates a repository of information about Voluntary Organisations (VOs) and Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs) across India. It acts as a bridge between NGOs and central government ministries and departments.

On successful registration, every NGO is issued a Unique ID used as primary identification when dealing with government bodies. The Unique ID is widely treated as mandatory for receiving grants, accessing CSR funding and participating in government schemes. The portal improves transparency and helps departments identify and partner with credible NGOs.

ELIGIBLE ORGANISATIONS

Who Can Register

01

Society

Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (or state Act). Minimum members as per law; charitable/social objectives.

02

Trust

Public charitable trust under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882 (or applicable state law). Registered Trust Deed and trustees.

03

Section 8 Company

Non-profit company under the Companies Act, 2013. Certificate of Incorporation and no dividend distribution.

04

Not Eligible

Political organisations, commercial entities and government bodies are not eligible for NGO Darpan registration.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Registration Certificate

Society Registration Certificate, Trust Deed or Section 8 Certificate of Incorporation.

2. NGO PAN

PAN in the name of the organisation (not personal PAN of founder/trustee).

3. Office Bearer ID

Aadhaar and PAN of authorised signatory (and typically other key office bearers).

4. Bank Details

Bank account in the NGO’s name — account number, IFSC and cancelled cheque/statement.

5. Address / Governing Docs

Office address proof and governing document (MoA/Rules, Trust Deed, MoA & AoA).

6. Optional Strengthening

12A/80G, FCRA, audited accounts or annual reports if available.

PROCESS

How NGO DARPAN Registration Works

1. Eligibility Check

Confirm the organisation is a registered Society, Trust or Section 8 with entity PAN and bank account.

2. Document Preparation

Gather registration certificate, PAN, Aadhaar of signatory, bank details and governing documents.

3. Portal Registration

Create account and submit the application on the NITI Aayog NGO Darpan portal (ngodarpan.gov.in).

4. Profile & Upload

Complete organisation profile, activities and upload documents in the required format.

5. Unique ID

Receive Unique ID after successful submission/verification. Lifetime validity while the NGO remains active.
