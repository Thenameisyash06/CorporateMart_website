OVERVIEW

What is a Contractor License?

A Contractor License (PWD Contractor Registration Certificate) is official registration issued by the State Public Works Department, Central Public Works Department (CPWD) or Municipal Corporation. It authorises an individual or firm to undertake building, civil, electrical, plumbing or road construction works of a specified value and to bid for government tenders.

Classification into Class A, B, C or D is based on financial standing (turnover, solvency), technical qualifications, past project experience and equipment. The license is class- and category-specific. Operating without a valid license on government works can lead to disqualification, EMD forfeiture, blacklisting and legal action under state contractor registration rules.

CLASSES

Indicative Class & Category Guide

Class Project Value (Indicative) Typical Requirements
Class A Above ₹1 crore Engineering degree, 5+ years, high turnover, solvency, equipment
Class B Up to ₹1 crore Degree/diploma, 3+ years, moderate turnover, solvency
Class C Up to ₹25 lakh Diploma/ITI, 2+ years, basic financial capacity
Class D Up to ₹5 lakh ITI or experience-based, basic capacity
Exact thresholds vary by state PWD rules and CPWD Manual. Categories include Civil, Electrical, Plumbing and Road. Always confirm with the applicable authority.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. Identity & Address

PAN, Aadhaar and address proof of the applicant or authorised signatory.

2. Qualifications

Engineering degree, diploma or ITI as required for the class and category.

3. Experience

Work completion certificates from contracting authorities for past projects.

4. Financials

Audited financial statements (typically 3 years) and bank solvency certificate.

5. Entity & GST

Certificate of Incorporation or firm registration, GST certificate where applicable.

6. EMD & Equipment

Earnest Money Deposit (DD/FDR/BG) and proof of equipment ownership as required by class.

PROCESS

How Contractor License Registration Works

1. Class & Category Assessment

Determine the appropriate class (A/B/C/D) and category based on capacity and experience.

2. Document Collection

Gather qualifications, experience certificates, audited accounts, solvency and EMD proof.

3. Application Filing

File on the State PWD / CPWD / Municipal portal (or offline as prescribed) with prescribed fees and EMD.

4. Verification

Authority verifies documents, financials and experience; may call for clarification or site verification.

5. Certificate

Registration certificate issued for the approved class and category; valid for 3–5 years. Renew before expiry.
