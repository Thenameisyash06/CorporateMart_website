OVERVIEW

What is Consent to Establish (CTE)?

Consent to Establish (CTE) is a primary statutory environmental permit issued by State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs) or Union Territory Pollution Control Committees (PCCs). Governed under Section 25 of the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974, and Section 21 of the Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1981, CTE approval must be secured prior to constructing, expanding, or installing any industrial plant, manufacturing unit, or commercial facility that generates trade effluent, air emissions, or hazardous waste.

Obtaining CTE validates that your proposed unit's location, site plan, pollution control equipment, and waste mitigation strategies align with statutory environmental standards. Corporate Mart manages the complete CTE cycle—from technical project report (DPR) preparation, Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) capacity modeling, and CPCB category audit to state portal filings and authority inspection defense.

CPCB CLASSIFICATION

Industrial Categorization for CTE

The Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) classifies industries into four color-coded categories based on their Pollution Index (PI) score, which dictates the complexity of CTE processing.

Red

Red Category (PI > 60)

Heavy polluting industries such as pharmaceuticals, tanneries, chemical manufacturing, distilleries, power plants, and electroplating units. Requires rigorous ETP/APCD reviews and EIA approvals.

Orange

Orange Category (PI 41–59)

Moderately polluting sectors including food processing, auto servicing, textile processing, hotels, building construction projects, and electronic component manufacturing.

Green

Green Category (PI 21–40)

Low polluting units such as small assembly plants, dry packaging, solar power plants, bakery units, flour mills, and light engineering operations with streamlined fast-track CTE processing.

White

White Category (PI up to 20)

Non-polluting industries like IT software parks, handlooms, assembly of air coolers, and bicycle manufacturing. Exempted from formal CTE/CTO, requiring only a simple self-declaration intimation.

REQUIREMENTS

Who Needs Consent to Establish (CTE)?

Any project or enterprise planning activities that impact air, water, or land environments must obtain CTE clearance before breaking ground.

Plant Expansion / Modification Required when expanding production capacity or altering product manufacturing lines. State SPCB / Regional Board Office
Health Care Establishments (HCE) Hospitals, diagnostic labs, and clinics generating bio-medical waste before setup. SPCB Bio-Medical Waste Cell

DOCUMENTATION

Required Documents for CTE Application

1. Business Legal Registration

Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, GST Registration Certificate, and PAN card of the business entity.

2. Land Ownership & Lease Documents

Sale Deed, Rent/Lease Agreement, Land Possession Certificate, and Revenue Land Use Classification (NA Clearance).

3. Detailed Site Plan & Layout Map

Architectural site layout showing factory sheds, raw material storage, hazard zones, green belt allocation, and ETP/STP locations.

4. Project Report & Process Flowchart

Detailed Project Report (DPR) explaining manufacturing processes, chemical reactions, raw material consumption, and daily water balances.

5. ETP / STP & Emission System Scheme

Engineering drawings, capacity design calculations, and technical specifications of Effluent/Sewage Treatment Plants and air pollution control devices.

6. Local Authority NOC & Authorizations

No Objection Certificate (NOC) from local Gram Panchayat or Municipal Corporation, and CGWA approval for groundwater abstraction (if applicable).

STEP-BY-STEP WORKFLOW

Consent to Establish Approval Roadmap

1. Industrial Profiling & Category Audit

Our environmental engineers analyze your manufacturing process, raw materials, water consumption, and emission points to classify your unit under CPCB guidelines.

2. Project Report & Pollution Scheme Designing

Drafting the Detailed Project Report (DPR), water mass balance diagrams, and technical specifications for Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP) or Air Pollution Control Devices (APCD).

3. Online SPCB OCMMS Portal Application

Compiling all documentation, completing state-specific online forms on the OCMMS portal, calculating capital investment fees, and submitting government fee payments.

4. Authority Field Inspection & Query Defense

Coordinating with SPCB Regional Officers during site inspections, addressing technical clarification letters, and providing representations before board consent committees.

5. Grant of CTE Certificate

Securing the official Consent to Establish certificate, enabling you to commence factory construction, machinery installation, and utility setups lawfully.

COMPLIANCE TIMELINE

CTE Validity, Extension & Transition to CTO

Understanding the life cycle of your CTE permit ensures uninterrupted project completion.

01

Standard CTE Validity

CTE certificates are typically granted for a period of 1 to 5 years depending on the state board and estimated project construction timeline.

02

CTE Extension

If factory construction or machinery commissioning is delayed, an application for CTE extension must be submitted to SPCB prior to certificate expiry.

03

Transition to CTO

Once construction is completed and pollution control devices are installed, units must apply for Consent to Operate (CTO) before trial production begins.
