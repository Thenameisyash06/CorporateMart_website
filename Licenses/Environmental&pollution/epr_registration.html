OVERVIEW

What is Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)?

Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) is an environmental policy approach under which entity producers, importers, and brand owners (PIBOs) are assigned significant financial and physical responsibility for the post-consumer disposal, recycling, and processing of products they introduce into the market.

Administered by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) and State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs) under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), EPR regulations mandate registration on dedicated portal portals before commercial operations or imports. Corporate Mart offers comprehensive solutions covering EPR registration, liability calculation, credit procurement from authorized recyclers, and annual compliance reporting across plastic, e-waste, battery, and tyre sectors.

REGULATORY SCOPE

EPR Regulated Waste Frameworks

India mandates EPR registration across four key environmental waste streams under specific CPCB frameworks.

01

Plastic Packaging Waste

Covers Category I (Rigid), Category II (Flexible single/multi-layer), Category III (Multi-layered plastic packaging), and Category IV (Compostable plastics) used in commercial product packaging.

02

E-Waste (Electrical & Electronics)

Encompasses 106+ items across IT & telecom hardware, consumer electronics, medical equipment, laboratory devices, and household electrical appliances under E-Waste Rules.

03

Waste Batteries

Applies to EV batteries, portable batteries, industrial batteries, and automotive lead-acid or lithium-ion energy storage systems sold across Indian markets.

04

Waste Tyres

Governs importers and domestic manufacturers of pneumatic tyres for two-wheelers, passenger vehicles, commercial haulage, aircraft, and heavy machinery equipment.

TARGET AUDIENCE

Who Needs EPR Registration? (PIBOs)

Any business operating in manufacturing, importing, or selling goods with regulated packaging or components must register.

01

Producers (P)

Domestic manufacturers involved in fabricating plastic packaging, electronic hardware, batteries, or pneumatic tyres intended for sale inside India.

02

Importers (I)

Businesses importing finished goods, packaged merchandise, raw plastic packaging materials, batteries, tyres, or IT hardware into Indian customs territories.

03

Brand Owners (BO)

E-commerce platforms, retail chains, or brand entities selling products under their own trademarked brand names using regulated packaging or components.

DOCUMENTATION

Required Documents for EPR Registration

1. Business Entity Proof

Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, GST Registration Certificate, and PAN card of the business unit.

2. Authorized Signatory KYC

Aadhaar card, PAN card, email ID, mobile number, and board resolution or authorization letter for the designated officer.

3. SPCB Consents (For Producers)

Valid Consent to Establish (CTE) and Consent to Operate (CTO) issued by the relevant State Pollution Control Board.

4. Import Export Code (For Importers)

IEC License issued by DGFT along with custom bill of entry records for past import quantity assessments.

5. Sales Data & Target Declarations

Audited year-wise sales statements, quantity of packaging material or electrical goods introduced into the market.

6. Process Flow & Packaging Specs

Detailed technical specifications of packaging materials (Category I-IV), product manuals, or hazardous material reports.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Any business operating as a Producer, Importer, or Brand Owner (PIBO) dealing with plastic packaging, electrical and electronic equipment (e-waste), waste batteries, or waste tyres must register on the CPCB portal before selling or importing goods into India.

If your manufacturing, distribution, or sales presence spans two or more states in India (or if you are an importer), registration must be filed directly with the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB). If operations are confined strictly within a single state, registration is handled by the respective State Pollution Control Board (SPCB).

Targets are computed based on the quantity of packaging material or products introduced into the market over previous financial years, adjusted by percentage metrics established in CPCB guidelines. Importers calculate targets based on custom bill of entry data.

Non-compliance attracts heavy Environmental Compensation (EC) charges imposed by CPCB under Section 15 of the Environment (Protection) Act. Penalties can run into lakhs or crores of rupees depending on unfulfilled target volumes, alongside potential customs clearance blocks.

EPR Registration Certificates granted by CPCB are generally valid for 1 to 5 years depending on the specific waste category (e.g., Plastic EPR certificates are typically valid for 1 year initially, while E-Waste registrations may be granted for up to 5 years subject to annual return filings).

Foreign entities cannot register directly. They must apply through an authorized Indian subsidiary, an appointed Indian Importer, or an Authorized Representative registered within India holding a valid GSTIN and PAN.
