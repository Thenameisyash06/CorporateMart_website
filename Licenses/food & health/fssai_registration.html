OVERVIEW

FSSAI License — Mandatory Food Safety Authorization

FSSAI Registration is a compulsory legal requirement for every Food Business Operator (FBO) in India under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. Enforced by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), this regulation applies across the entire food supply chain—including small home kitchens, food stalls, cloud kitchens, restaurants, repackers, cold storage units, food importers, and large-scale manufacturing facilities.

Upon successful registration through the Food Safety Compliance System (FoSCoS) portal, every FBO receives a unique 14-digit registration or license number. This number serves as official proof that the food produced, processed, or handled adheres to strict national hygiene, quality, and consumer safety standards. Running a food business without a valid FSSAI certificate is a punishable offense leading to heavy fines, product seizures, or immediate business shutdown.

Governing ActFood Safety & Standards Act, 2006
Regulatory AuthorityFSSAI & State Food Safety Depts

Validity Period1 to 5 Years (Choice based)
Processing PortalForm A (Basic) / Form B (State & Central)

Three Tiers of FSSAI Food Licenses

Selecting the correct Kind of Business (KoB) and license tier prevents application rejections, penalties, and operational delays.

License Tier Turnover / Scope Criteria Issuing Authority Suitable For
Basic FSSAI Registration Turnover up to ₹12 Lakhs per annum Registering Authority / Municipal Officers Petty food manufacturers, hawkers, small retailers, home-based tiffin centers, small bakeries, and food stalls.
State FSSAI License Turnover between ₹12 Lakhs to ₹20 Crores per annum State Food Safety Authority (DO/FSO) Mid-sized manufacturers, cloud kitchens, restaurants, caterers, distributors, storage facilities, and mid-level hotels.
Central FSSAI License Turnover above ₹20 Crores, or Multi-State/Import-Export operations Central FSSAI Licensing Authority Large food processors, 5-star hotels, airport/seaport food vendors, food importers/exporters, and multi-state chain operators.

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

Document Checklist by License Category

Basic Registration Checklist

Passport-size photo of the proprietor/applicant, Aadhaar Card or Voter ID, proof of business address (Rent Agreement/Electricity Bill), and basic photo ID.

State License Checklist

Identity proof, business address proof, Constitution proof (GST, Partnership Deed, COI), list of food categories handled, blueprints/layout plan of the unit, and Machinery list (for manufacturers).

Central License Checklist

All State documents plus Water Testing Analysis Report, Form IX (Nomination of Authorized Representative), IEC certificate (for importers/exporters), and NOC from local municipal body/pollution board.

Manufacturer & Repacker Extras

List of equipment and machinery with installed capacity, layout plan showing workflow dimensions, and raw material procurement source validation.
