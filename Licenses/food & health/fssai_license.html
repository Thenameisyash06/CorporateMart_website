OVERVIEW

FSSAI LICENSE - AN OVERVIEW

FSSAI License is a mandatory registration for all Food Business Operators (FBOs) in India under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. Issued by the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI), it certifies that your food business complies with prescribed hygiene, safety, and operational standards.

Every entity involved in manufacturing, processing, packaging, storage, distribution, transportation, e-commerce, or retail of food items must obtain an FSSAI license or registration. The 14-digit FSSAI License number must be prominently displayed on all food packaging and at operational premises, helping consumers trace quality and building vital brand credibility.

Governing BodyFSSAI (Ministry of Health & Family Welfare)
Governing ActFood Safety and Standards Act, 2006
Identifier14-Digit FSSAI License / Registration No.
Validity Period1 to 5 Years (Renewable 30 days before expiry)
Application PortalFoSCoS (Food Safety Compliance System)
ScopeMandatory for all FBOs operating in India
LICENSE CATEGORIES

Types of FSSAI Licenses

Choose the correct FSSAI license category based on your business operations, scale, and annual turnover.

License Type Annual Turnover Validity Suitable For
Basic Registration Up to ₹12 Lakhs 1 - 5 Years Small businesses, petty retailers, home-based food units, hawkers, tiffin services, small bakeries.
State License ₹12 Lakhs to ₹20 Crores 1 - 5 Years Medium manufacturers, restaurants, hotels, caterers, distributors, storage units operating within a single state.
Central License Above ₹20 Crores 1 - 5 Years Large food manufacturers, importers, exporters, multi-state chains, 100% EOUs, and e-commerce portals.

*Important Note: Central License is mandatory irrespective of turnover for (i) Food importers and exporters, (ii) Businesses operating across 2 or more states, (iii) 100% Export Oriented Units (EOUs), and (iv) Operations at seaports/airports.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

FSSAI Documentation Checklist

Ensure quick approval by preparing the required document set based on your license category.

1. Identity & Address Proof

PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, or Passport of the proprietor, partners, or directors of the food enterprise.

2. Business Premises Proof

Rental agreement, electricity bill, tax receipt, or NOC from the property owner for the operating location.

3. Photo & Entity Registration

Passport-size photographs along with Certificate of Incorporation, Partnership Deed, or Shop & Establishment License.

4. Product List & Categories

Detailed list of food products proposed to be manufactured, processed, stored, or sold with FSSAI food category codes.

5. Premises Layout & Blueprint (State/Central)

Scaled floor plan showing operational areas, machinery arrangement, and dimensions (required for manufacturing units).

6. Equipment & Water Test Report (State/Central)

List of machinery with installed horsepower and a NABL-accredited laboratory report for potable water used in production.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about FSSAI licenses and compliance.

Yes, FSSAI registration is mandatory for all food business operators, including small-scale home kitchens, bakeries, cloud kitchens, and tiffin services. Home businesses with an annual turnover up to ₹12 Lakhs can apply for a simple FSSAI Basic Registration (Form A).

Operating a food business without a valid FSSAI registration or license is a punishable offense under Section 63 of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. It can attract imprisonment for up to 6 months and a monetary penalty up to ₹5,000,000.

The processing timeline depends on the license category: Basic Registration usually takes 7 to 15 working days; State License takes 30 to 45 working days; and Central License takes 45 to 60 working days, depending on document readiness and inspector availability.

FSSAI Registration (Form A) is meant for petty food businesses with an annual turnover below ₹12 Lakhs. FSSAI License (Form B) is required for medium to large enterprises and is sub-divided into State License (turnover ₹12 Lakhs to ₹20 Crores) and Central License (turnover exceeding ₹20 Crores or specialized operations like imports/multi-state facilities).

Yes, you can apply for an FSSAI License Modification on the FoSCoS portal at any time to add new food product categories, change business premises, update contact information, or increase production capacity without applying for a fresh license from scratch.

FSSAI Licenses can be renewed for a term of 1 to 5 years and must be filed at least 30 days prior to the expiration date printed on the certificate. Late filings attract a penalty fee of ₹100 for every day of delay past the expiration date.

Yes, all online food platforms and e-commerce aggregators strictly require an active 14-digit FSSAI License or Registration certificate before onboarding your food venture or storefront onto their platform.
