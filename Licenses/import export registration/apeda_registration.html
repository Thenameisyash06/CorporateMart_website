OVERVIEW

What is APEDA Registration?

APEDA Registration is mandatory registration issued by the Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA), a statutory body under the APEDA Act 1985. Every exporter of agricultural and processed food products listed in the Schedule to the Act must obtain this registration before commencing exports.

APEDA promotes and regulates 14 scheduled product groups — fruits and vegetables, meat, dairy, cereals, floriculture, honey, cocoa, beverages, groundnuts, pickles, guar gum, herbal and medicinal plants, and more. Registration unlocks financial assistance, TraceNet, quality standards support and trade promotion. It is separate from IEC; you need both.

Authority APEDA · Ministry of Commerce

WHO NEEDS IT

Who Must Register with APEDA

01

Fruit & Vegetable Exporters

Fresh, frozen, dried fruits and vegetables, pulps and juices — often with TraceNet for grapes.

02

Meat, Dairy & Cereals

Buffalo/sheep/goat/poultry meat, milk products, basmati and non-basmati rice, millets and cereals.

03

Floriculture & Organic

Cut flowers, dried flowers, organic products under NPOP with Organic TraceNet.

04

Processed & Other Scheduled

Pickles, honey, guar gum, groundnuts, cocoa, herbal/medicinal plants and other scheduled items.

PRODUCTS

14 Scheduled Product Groups

01

Fruits & Vegetables

Fresh, frozen, dried and processed — mangoes, grapes, onions, pulps, juices.

02

Meat & Dairy

Meat and meat products; milk, cheese, butter, ghee and related dairy items.

03

Cereals & Floriculture

Basmati/non-basmati rice, wheat, millets; cut flowers, dried flowers, foliage.

04

Others

Honey, cocoa, beverages, groundnuts, pickles, guar gum, herbal and medicinal plants.

Note: Spices (Spice Board), tea, coffee and marine products (MPEDA) fall outside APEDA and need separate registrations.

DOCUMENTS

What You Typically Need

1. IEC Certificate

Valid Import Export Code from DGFT (mandatory prerequisite).

2. GST Certificate

Active GSTIN of the exporting entity.

3. PAN & Entity Docs

PAN of firm/company; COI, Partnership Deed or LLP Agreement.

4. Bank Details

Cancelled cheque or bank certificate for the entity account.

5. RCMC (if applicable)

Registration Cum Membership Certificate from the relevant EPC where required.

6. Address & Authorisation

Office address proof and authorisation letter for the signatory.

PROCESS

How APEDA Registration Works

1. IEC Ready

Ensure you have a valid Import Export Code from DGFT.

2. Document Prep

Gather IEC, GST, PAN, entity documents, bank details and authorisation.

3. Portal Application

Create account and file online application on the APEDA portal (apeda.gov.in).

4. Fee & Verification

Pay government fee (typically ₹5,000) and respond to any queries from APEDA.

5. Certificate & TraceNet

Obtain registration certificate; set up TraceNet (GrapeNet / Organic) where product requires it.
