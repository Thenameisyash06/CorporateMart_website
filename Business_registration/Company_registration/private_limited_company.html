OVERVIEW

What Is a Private Limited Company?

A Private Limited Company is a closely held business structure incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013. It operates as a separate legal entity and can have between 2 and 200 members.

Incorporation is handled through the MCA's integrated SPICe+ process. Depending on the application, linked registrations can include DIN, PAN, TAN, GSTIN, EPFO and ESIC.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Private Limited Company?+

It is a closely held corporate structure with a separate legal identity and limited liability, subject to the Companies Act and applicable rules.

How many directors are required?+

A minimum of two directors is required, with at least one director meeting the applicable Indian residency requirement.

How many shareholders are required?+

A Private Limited Company generally requires at least two shareholders and can have up to 200 members under the applicable definition.

Is there a minimum paid-up capital requirement?+

There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement for incorporation, although the capital structure still needs to be properly declared.

What is SPICe+?+

SPICe+ is the MCA's integrated incorporation process used for company name reservation, incorporation and linked registrations.

Can NRIs or foreign nationals become directors?+

Yes, subject to applicable DIN, DSC, passport and document authentication requirements, while the resident-director requirement must also be satisfied.

What happens after incorporation?+

The company must continue with applicable statutory filings, tax compliance, director KYC, annual returns and other event-based obligations.
