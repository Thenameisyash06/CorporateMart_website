OVERVIEW

What is Startup India Registration (DPIIT Recognition)?

Startup India Registration is the process of obtaining official DPIIT recognition for an entity incorporated as a Private Limited Company, LLP, or Partnership Firm, enabling access to Section 80-IAC tax holiday, IPR fee rebates, self-certification benefits, and government procurement relaxations under the Startup India initiative. The DPIIT, under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, administers this through the Startup India portal (startupindia.gov.in).

Launched on 16 January 2016, the initiative has 3 pillars: Simplification & Handholding, Funding & Incentives, and Industry-Academia Partnership. Over 1,40,000 startups have been recognized, creating 12 lakh+ direct jobs across 763 districts.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What you need to apply.

1. Certificate of Incorporation

Pvt Ltd / OPC Certificate from MCA, LLP Incorporation Certificate, or Partnership Deed Registration Certificate.

2. PAN Card of Entity

Entity PAN mandatory. Director/Partner PAN details also needed for portal login.

3. Company/LLP Documents

MOA + AOA (for Pvt Ltd / OPC) or LLP Agreement (for LLP) or Partnership Deed.

4. Proof of Funding (if any)

Funding letter, term sheet, or SEBI-registered AIF certificate if applicable.

5. Awards & Recognition (if any)

Any government awards, patents filed, or BIRAC/DST funding letters that support the application.

6. Innovation Write-up / PoC

Self-declared innovation narrative explaining the product/service, its novelty, and scalability.

5-STEP TIMELINE

The Application Process

1. Eligibility Check

Confirm entity type, age, turnover cap, and innovation nature. Register on startupindia.gov.in.

2. Portal Registration

Create DPIIT account using entity email. Verify OTP and complete profile.

3. Application Filing

Fill Form-2 (Application for DPIIT Recognition). Upload entity documents, innovation PoC, and supporting materials.

4. DPIIT Review

DPIIT reviews application (typically 3–7 working days). May ask for clarifications.

5. Recognition Certificate

DPIIT issues Recognition Certificate with a unique Startup Recognition Number (SRN). Access all benefits.
