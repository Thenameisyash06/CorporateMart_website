Qualified B2B & B2C Leads, Not Cold Lists.

Inbound funnels that capture high-intent inquiries, route them to your CRM and nurture non-responders — so your sales team talks to people who asked to be contacted.

CorporateMart builds lead generation systems with multi-step qualification forms, Google/Meta traffic, instant WhatsApp & email alerts, CRM sync (Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce or Sheets) and automated nurture sequences. Ad spend stays on your card; our fee covers funnel, automation and optimisation.

Unlike bought databases, every lead is exclusive and opt-in — prospects who engaged with your offer and submitted their details.
