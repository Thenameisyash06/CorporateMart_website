This Privacy Policy forms an integral part of our Terms of Use and shall govern your interaction with our Services. By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge and agree to the terms set forth in this Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are text files containing small amounts of information which are downloaded to your computer or mobile device when you visit a website and allow a website to recognize your device. They enable the website owner to distinguish you from other users of the website. Cookies cannot be executed as code or used to deliver viruses and they cannot enable us to access your hard drive. We cannot read any information on your hard drive even if we store cookies there.

How do we use cookies and related technologies?

Cookies do many jobs, such as allowing visitors to navigate between web pages efficiently, memorizing visitor's preferences and mostly refining visitors browsing experience. They may also help to ensure that the advertisements you see online are more appropriate to you and your interests. We use cookies for a number of reasons, like helping us see which features are most popular, counting visitors to a page, improving our users' experience, keeping our services secure, and generally providing you with a better experience. The cookies we use generally fall into one of the following categories.

CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. might use data collected from cookies to identify registered and unregistered users behavior and to serve them with content and promotions based on their profile, interest, and for the other purposes described below, to the extent legally acceptable in certain jurisdictions.

Some of the cookies CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. use, does not gathers information that identifies a visitor. For example -

  • Performance cookies (see below)
  • Targeting cookies (see below) where you are not a registered user.

In other cases, we may associate cookie information (including information from cookies placed via our advertisements through third party Services or any other source) with an identifiable individual. For example -

If we send you an email contains web cookies, beacons or similar technologies, we will know whether you open, read, or delete the message. When you click a link in a marketing e-mail you receive from CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd., we will also use a cookie to log what pages you view and what content you download from our web Services, even if you are not registered at or signed into our site.

Merging and analyzing your personal data - As mentioned above, we might merge data taken from public sources, from different mails, web Services, and personal communications with you (this includes all details collected across CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. Web Services such as information collected when you sign-up or log in to our web Services or connect to our web Services using your social media credentials (such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook etc.). We combine this information to better assess your experience with CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. and to perform the other activities described in our privacy policy.

Do you use any cookies from third party companies?

Some cookies we use are from third party companies, such as Google Analytics, Adobe Analytics, Marketo Munchkin Tracking, Google remarketing, Hubspot, Linkedin Analytics etc. to provide us with web analytics and Al about our Services. These companies use code to collect data about your interaction with our Services, for example the pages you visit, the links or hyperlink you click on and how much time you spent on our web Services. This code is only active while you are on CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. website. For more information on how these CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. partners collect and use information on CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. behalf, please refer to their privacy policies: Google at Google Privacy & Terms, Adobe at Adobe Privacy Center, Marketo at Marketo Privacy Policy, Hubspot at Hubspot Privacy Policy, As Remarketing at Advanced Remarketing Services, Linkedin at Linkedin Privacy Policy, and Yoptima at Yoptima Privacy Policy.

Does CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. use any non-cookie tracking technologies?

CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd.. and its associate-partners might also use web beacons (including conversion pixels) or other similar technologies for similar purposes as above and we might include these on our web Services, in E-mail marketing, newsletter and affiliated web Services, to determine whether messages have been opened and links clicked on. Web beacons do not place information on browsing device, but they may work in connections with cookies to monitor website activity. The details provided below about cookies also applies to web beacons and other similar technologies. Conversion pixels are small codes located on a specific webpage which are activated when someone visits a page resulting in an increase in the conversion count.

What if I don't want cookies?

By using our website, you agree that we can place cookies on your device as mentioned below. If you want to remove or delete existing cookies from your device you can do this with your browser options. If you want to block future cookies being placed on your device, you can do this by changing your browser settings. For more information on managing cookies see Cookies and Similar Technologies. Currently, "Do Not Track" initiatives are not recognized by our site. If you refuse, we ensure that cookies will not be placed on your machine.

Please note that removing, deleting and blocking cookies might have an impact on your user experience. Unless you have edited your browser settings to block cookies, our system will issue cookies as soon as you visit our site or click on a link in an email that we have sent you, even if you have previously deleted or removed our cookies.

However, it is important to note that not all cookies might be used in all jurisdictions or web Services. A list of all the cookies used on CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. website by category is set out below. There are four types of cookies categories as general and within these four categories, they are further classified as either session or persistent cookies.

  • Session Cookies: "Session" cookies are temporary cookies and once you close the browser window, they are auto deleted from your device.
  • Persistent Cookies: "Persistent" cookies remain on your device for a longer period of time and are used by the website to identify your device when you return.

CorporateMart Services Pvt. Ltd. uses both session and persistent cookies.

07

Sharing of Information with Third Parties

Strict policy: No unauthorized selling or renting of personal information

Zero Commercial Data Reselling

CorporateMart does not sell or rent personal information. However, information may be shared under the following circumstances:

  1. Order Processing and Service Delivery: Personal information may be shared with trusted third-party service providers or affiliates to process payments, complete transactions, and deliver requested services. Such information may also be used to communicate updates, respond to inquiries, and provide service-related information.
  2. Registered Agent or Authorized Representatives: If a user designates a third party (such as a registered agent or representative), relevant contact details may be shared with such party upon request for service fulfillment.
  3. Third-Party Marketing: Personal information is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes without explicit user consent. If a user opts for a third-party offer or bundled service, necessary information may be shared strictly in connection with that specific request.
  4. Legal and Regulatory Disclosure: We may disclose personal information when required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request, or where necessary to:
    • Protect our legal rights
    • Prevent fraud or illegal activity
    • Ensure safety and security
  5. Aggregated and Demographic Data: We may use collected data to generate aggregated insights and general statistics about users. Such data does not identify any individual and may be used for analytical or business purposes.
  6. Professional and Legal Services: With user consent, information may be shared with professionals such as legal advisors or service partners to facilitate service delivery.
  7. Service Providers: We may share personal information with authorized service providers who assist in operations, marketing, or support services. These entities are contractually obligated to use such data only for specified purposes and maintain confidentiality.
  8. Partner and Affiliate Tracking: CorporateMart may use third-party tools (such as analytics and advertising platforms) that collect non-personal information through cookies and tracking technologies to provide relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness.
  9. Public Forums and User-Generated Content: Our platform may include blogs, forums, or other publicly accessible areas. Any information shared in such areas may be visible to other users. Users are advised to exercise caution when sharing personal information. Requests for removal of such information may be submitted; however, complete removal may not always be possible.
  10. Business Transfers and Corporate Changes: In the event of merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, user information may be transferred as part of the business transaction. Users will be notified of such changes and provided with available choices regarding their data.
  11. Insolvency or Legal Proceedings: In situations involving insolvency, bankruptcy, or similar legal proceedings, user data may be treated as a business asset and may be transferred or shared in accordance with applicable laws. Users will be informed of any such developments as required.
  12. Third-Party Websites and External Links: Our website may contain links to external websites. This Privacy Policy applies only to CorporateMart Services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites, and users are advised to review their respective policies. Our platform may include social media integrations (such as sharing tools or widgets) that may collect certain technical data, including IP address and browsing activity. Such features are governed by the privacy policies of the respective third-party providers.
