FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are event-based ROC compliances? +

Mandatory filings triggered by a specific corporate event — such as director change, office shift, share allotment or capital increase — rather than by a fixed annual calendar. Forms must be filed with the ROC on the MCA portal within the prescribed period from the event date.

What is Form DIR-12 used for? +

DIR-12 reports appointment, resignation or change in particulars of a director. It must be filed within 30 days of the event. Supporting DIR-2 consent and board resolution are typically required for appointments.

What is the late filing penalty? +

Additional fee of ₹100 per day under Section 403 applies for many event-based forms, with no maximum cap. Filing early after the event is the safest way to avoid large costs.

How long does filing take? +

With complete documents and DSC, professional processing is often in the 5–15 working day range, subject to MCA processing and any queries from the ROC.

Do I need a special resolution? +

Some events (e.g. authorised capital increase, name change, certain office shifts) require a special resolution (75% voting rights). Others can be done by board resolution alone. We confirm the correct type for your event.

Is this the same as annual compliance? +

No. Annual compliance (AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-3 KYC) follows fixed yearly deadlines linked to the AGM. Event-based filings are one-off and tied to the date of the corporate change.
