OVERVIEW

What Are Accounting Services?

Accounting services cover professional financial record-keeping, statement preparation, tax compliance and regulatory support for businesses. A dedicated finance professional records and classifies transactions, prepares monthly Profit & Loss and Balance Sheet, computes GST and TDS, reconciles bank accounts and supports statutory returns.

Under Section 128 of the Companies Act, 2013, every company must maintain proper books of account. Similar obligations apply to LLPs and to proprietors above prescribed thresholds under Section 44AA of the Income Tax Act. We act as your accounting service provider only — we do not act as statutory auditor. Section 144 bars the same person from providing both accounting and statutory audit to the same company, so keeping the two roles separate is a legal requirement.
