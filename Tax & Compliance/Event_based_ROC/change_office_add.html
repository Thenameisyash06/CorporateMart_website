OVERVIEW

What Is a Registered Office Address Change?

A registered office address change is the statutory process of updating a company’s official address with the Registrar of Companies by filing Form INC-22 under Section 12 of the Companies Act, 2013. Every company must file this form within 15 to 30 days of relocating its registered office.

The process varies based on four scenarios: within the same city, between cities in the same state and ROC, between ROC jurisdictions within the same state, and between states. Government fee starts at ₹200 for companies with authorised capital up to ₹1 lakh. Section 12(3)(a) requires a name board at the registered office displaying the company name, CIN, address, email and phone.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

Documents Required for Registered Office Change

1. Board / Special Resolution

Board resolution for within-city change. Special resolution (75% majority) for inter-city, inter-ROC and inter-state changes.

2. Utility Bill

Electricity, telephone or gas bill for the new address, not older than 2 months from the date of INC-22 filing.

3. NOC from Landlord

No Objection Certificate from the landlord if the premises is rented, matching the exact address in the form.

4. Rent Agreement / Ownership Proof

Valid rent agreement or ownership documents for the new registered office premises.

5. Office Photo with Name Board

Photograph showing the name board (company name, CIN, address, email, phone) with a director present and geolocation metadata.

6. Additional for Inter-State

Amended MoA, newspaper notice (English + vernacular) and creditor consent letters.

PROCESS

Step-by-Step Process to Change Registered Office

1. Pass Board or Special Resolution

For within-city: board resolution. For inter-city / inter-ROC / inter-state: special resolution with 75% shareholder majority at EGM (21-day notice under Section 101).

2. Arrange Address Proof and NOC

Obtain utility bill (≤ 2 months old), landlord NOC (if rented), rent agreement/ownership proof and office photograph with name board and geolocation.

3. File INC-22 on MCA V3 Portal

Fill Form INC-22 with new address details, attach documents, get professional certification, affix director DSC and pay government fee (₹200–₹5,000). Submit within 15 days (within city) or 30 days (inter-city).

4. File MGT-14 (If Applicable)

If a special resolution was passed, file Form MGT-14 within 30 days of the resolution. Attach special resolution and explanatory statement.

5. File INC-23 for RD Approval (If Applicable)

For inter-ROC or inter-state: file INC-23 with Regional Director (₹5,000 fee). Attach resolution, newspaper ad (inter-state), creditor consent and altered MoA. Processing 30–180 days.

6. Receive ROC Approval & Updated Records

ROC updates Master Data. For INC-23 cases, file INC-22 with the new ROC after RD order. Download updated Certificate of Incorporation.

7. Complete Post-Change Compliance

Update GST (within 15 days), PAN/TAN, banks, PF/ESI, letterheads and name board. Fresh GST registration if moving to a different state.

Penalty period starts from the date of the board resolution, not the date of physical relocation. A 15-day delay costs ₹15,000; a 30-day delay costs ₹30,000.

POST-CHANGE

Post-Change Compliance Checklist

01

GST Registration Amendment

File amendment on GST portal within 15 days. Highest priority — attracts Section 122 CGST penalties if missed. Fresh GST registration if moving to a different state.

02

PAN / TAN Update

File correction with NSDL / UTIITSL for PAN and TAN to reflect the new registered office address.

03

Bank, PF & ESI

Update bank account records and PF/ESI authorities (15-day deadline for PF/ESI) as soon as possible.

04

Name Board & Stationery

Display name board at new office (company name, CIN, address, email, phone) before filing INC-22. Update letterheads and stationery after approval.

COMPARISON

INC-22 vs INC-23: Which Form Do You Need?

Parameter INC-22 INC-23
Purpose Notify ROC of address change Seek Regional Director approval
Filed With Registrar of Companies Regional Director
Required For All types of office change Inter-ROC and inter-state only
Government Fee ₹200 – ₹5,000 (capital-based) ₹5,000 (flat fee)
Filing Deadline 15–30 days from resolution Before INC-22 (for inter-ROC/inter-state)
Professional Certification Required Not required
Processing Time 2 to 5 working days 30 to 180 days

Every registered office change requires INC-22. You need INC-23 only when the move crosses ROC jurisdiction or state boundaries. When both are needed, INC-23 is filed first.
