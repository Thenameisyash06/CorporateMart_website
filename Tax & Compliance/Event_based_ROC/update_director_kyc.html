OVERVIEW

What Is Director KYC Update?

Director KYC Update is the mandatory annual verification of personal details filed by every DIN holder through Form DIR-3 KYC or DIR-3 KYC-WEB on the MCA portal, required under Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014.

Every individual allotted a Director Identification Number (DIN) as of 31 March of any financial year must complete KYC before the due date, regardless of whether they hold an active directorship. Non-compliance results in automatic DIN deactivation and a ₹5,000 penalty.

EFORM VS WEB

DIR-3 KYC eForm vs DIR-3 KYC-WEB

Feature DIR-3 KYC (eForm) DIR-3 KYC-WEB
Filing Method Downloadable eForm with DSC Online web form with OTP
DSC Required Yes (mandatory) No
Professional Certification Yes No
Detail Changes Allowed Yes (mobile, email, address) No (verification only)
First-Time Filing Yes No

Decision guide: First-time / deactivated DIN / detail changes / foreign director → use eForm. Active DIN, no changes, repeat filer → use WEB. When in doubt, choose the eForm.

FILING PROCESS

Step-by-Step DIR-3 KYC Filing Process

1. Log In to MCA V3 Portal

Visit mca.gov.in and log in with Business User credentials. Navigate to MCA Services → Company e-Filing → DIR-3 KYC or DIR-3 KYC-WEB.

2. Select the Correct Form Type

Choose eForm for first-time, detail changes or reactivation. Choose WEB for annual KYC with no changes and active DIN.

3. Enter DIN and Verify Personal Details

Enter your 8-digit DIN. System auto-populates existing details. Verify name, DOB, PAN, Aadhaar/passport, nationality and address. Update if using eForm.

4. Complete OTP Verification

Enter OTPs sent to Aadhaar-linked mobile and registered email. Both OTPs must be entered within the validity period.

5. Upload Documents and Attach DSC (eForm Only)

Upload address proof, photograph and any updated documents. Attach director’s DSC. Practising professional also attaches DSC and provides certification.

6. Submit Form and Note SRN

Review and submit. MCA generates a unique Service Request Number (SRN) for tracking. WEB processes instantly; eForm takes 1–2 working days.

7. Download Acknowledgment and Verify DIN Status

Download filing acknowledgment. After processing, verify DIN status shows “Approved” using Check DIN Status on mca.gov.in. Retain SRN for records.

PENALTIES

Penalties for Late DIR-3 KYC Filing

01

DIN Deactivation

Automatic on 1 November 2025 (day after deadline). Director cannot sign MCA forms or accept new board positions.

02

₹5,000 Penalty

Non-negotiable government fee charged on reactivation filing. Applies per DIN, not per company.

03

Company Filing Block

AOC-4, MGT-7 and board resolution forms can be blocked until DIN is reactivated.

04

Cascading Penalties

Delayed AOC-4 / MGT-7 attracts ₹100 per day under Sections 137 / 92. Early filing prevents operational disruption.

DIN REACTIVATION

How to Reactivate a Deactivated DIN

01

File DIR-3 KYC eForm

Only the eForm (not WEB) can be used for reactivation. DSC and professional certification are mandatory.

02

Pay ₹5,000 Penalty

Government penalty of ₹5,000 per DIN must be paid along with the eForm submission.

03

OTP & Document Verification

Complete Aadhaar/passport OTP and upload required documents. Processing typically takes 1–2 working days.

04

Verify Approved Status

After processing, check DIN status on MCA portal. Once “Approved”, the director can again sign forms and hold directorships.
