OVERVIEW

What is Form ADT-1?

Form ADT-1 is a mandatory e-form filed with the Registrar of Companies to intimate the appointment of a statutory auditor under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 4(2) of the Companies (Audit and Auditors) Rules, 2014. Every company must file it within 15 days of the appointment date.

The form captures company CIN, auditor details (individual or firm), membership number, FRN, term of appointment and written consent. It covers first auditor (after Board resolution within 30 days of incorporation), subsequent appointment/reappointment at AGM, and casual vacancy. Processing is STP (straight-through) with auto-approval and SRN generation.

DUE DATE

When to File ADT-1

01

First Auditor

Board appoints within 30 days of incorporation. File ADT-1 within 15 days of the Board Resolution. (Mandatory for companies after 14 July 2025 amendment.)

02

Subsequent / Reappointment

Shareholders appoint/reappoint at AGM. File ADT-1 within 15 days of the AGM date.

03

Casual Vacancy

Board or general meeting fills vacancy. File ADT-1 within 15 days of the resolution appointing the new auditor.

04

If Board Misses 30 Days

EGM must appoint the first auditor within 90 days of incorporation; then file ADT-1 within 15 days of that appointment.

2025 AMENDMENT

G.S.R. 359(E) — Effective 14 July 2025

01

First Auditor ADT-1 Mandatory

ADT-1 is explicitly required for first auditor appointments. File within 15 days of Board Resolution. Prescribed fee ₹300 for this filing in many cases.

02

Revised Form Format

Form updated with additional fields for first auditor details. Use the current MCA form version.

03

ADT-4 Electronic

Fraud reporting (ADT-4) moved to mandatory electronic filing.

04

Practical Impact

Companies incorporated after 14 July 2025 should plan Board appointment within 30 days and ADT-1 within the next 15 days (about 45 days from incorporation).

PROCESS

How We File ADT-1

1. Eligibility & Consent

Verify auditor eligibility under Section 141. Obtain written consent and eligibility certificate from the auditor.

2. Resolution

Board resolution (first auditor / casual vacancy) or AGM resolution (subsequent/reappointment). Draft minutes as needed.

3. Prepare Form

Fill ADT-1 with CIN, auditor details, FRN, term and attachments (consent, certificate, resolution).

4. Submit on MCA

Affix DSC, pay government fee, submit on MCA V3. STP processing — SRN and acknowledgment typically on submission.

5. Confirm

Share SRN and filing acknowledgment. Retain for audit and annual compliance records.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What We Need

1. Auditor Consent

Written consent of the auditor (individual or firm) to act as statutory auditor.

2. Section 141 Certificate

Certificate that the auditor is eligible and not disqualified under Section 141.

3. Board / AGM Resolution

Certified copy of the resolution appointing or reappointing the auditor.

4. Auditor Details

Name, membership number, FRN (if firm), address and term of appointment.

5. Company Details

CIN, authorised capital (for fee), and authorised signatory DSC.

6. DSC

Digital signature of the director authorised to sign the form.
