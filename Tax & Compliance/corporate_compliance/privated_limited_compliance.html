OVERVIEW

What is Annual Compliance for a Pvt Ltd?

Annual compliance for a private limited company is the set of mandatory filings, meetings and audits every Pvt Ltd must complete each financial year under the Companies Act, 2013 to maintain active status with the Registrar of Companies.

Core obligations include filing AOC-4 (financial statements) and MGT-7 (annual return) with the ROC, conducting at least 4 board meetings (gap ≤ 120 days), holding an AGM within 6 months of FY end, completing DIR-3 KYC for all directors by 30 September, appointing/ re-appointing the auditor (ADT-1), filing ITR-6 and meeting GST/TDS obligations. Non-compliance attracts ₹100/day per form (uncapped for AOC-4/MGT-7), DIN deactivation and possible director disqualification under Section 164(2) after 3 consecutive years of default.

ROC FILINGS

Core MCA Forms

01

AOC-4 — Financial Statements

Balance sheet, P&L, cash flow, Board’s report and auditor’s report. File within 30 days of AGM. Late fee ₹100/day (uncapped).

02

MGT-7 / MGT-7A — Annual Return

Company particulars, directors, shareholding and compliance status. Within 60 days of AGM. Small companies/OPCs may use MGT-7A.

03

DIR-3 KYC

Annual KYC for every director by 30 September. Miss it and DIN can be deactivated; late fee ₹5,000 to reactivate.

04

ADT-1 — Auditor

Intimation of appointment/re-appointment of statutory auditor within 15 days of AGM.

MEETINGS

Board Meetings & AGM

01

Board Meetings (Section 173)

Minimum 4 board meetings in a calendar year. Gap between two consecutive meetings must not exceed 120 days. Minutes to be maintained.

02

AGM (Section 96)

Hold within 6 months of the end of the financial year — typically by 30 September for FY ending 31 March. Approve accounts, appoint auditor, etc.

03

MBP-1 & DIR-8

Disclosure of interest (MBP-1) and director declaration (DIR-8) at the first board meeting of the financial year (or as required).

04

Statutory Registers

Maintain registers of members, directors, charges, etc., and keep them updated with filings and meeting decisions.

COMPLIANCE CALENDAR

Typical Year (FY Ending 31 March)

April – June

Close books; start/complete statutory audit; prepare Directors’ Report and financial statements.

July – August

Board meeting to approve audited financials and Board’s Report; plan AGM.

September

Hold AGM (by 30 Sep); file DIR-3 KYC for all directors by 30 Sep; tax audit where applicable.

October

File AOC-4 (within 30 days of AGM); ADT-1 (within 15 days of AGM); ITR-6 by 31 Oct (with audit).

November

File MGT-7 / MGT-7A (within 60 days of AGM — e.g. by ~28/29 Nov if AGM on 30 Sep).

December

File GSTR-9 (if applicable) by 31 Dec; continue board meeting schedule for the year.
