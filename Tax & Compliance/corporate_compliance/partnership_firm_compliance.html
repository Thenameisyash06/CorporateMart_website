OVERVIEW

What is Partnership Firm Annual Compliance?

Annual compliance for a partnership firm under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 is entirely tax-side. There is no annual return to any registrar, no ROC form and no MCA fee. The Registrar of Firms is notified only when the constitution of the firm changes.

The firm must maintain books under Section 44AA, get a Section 44AB tax audit if turnover or professional receipts cross the prescribed thresholds, file ITR-5 by 31 July (non-audit) or 31 October (audit), deduct Section 194T TDS at 10% on partner payments above ₹20,000, and file GST/TDS returns if registered. Partner remuneration and interest are deductible only within Section 40(b) limits and as authorised by the partnership deed.

TAXATION

How a Partnership Firm Is Taxed

01

Flat 30% at Firm Level

The firm is taxed at a flat 30% on its total income. Partner's share of profit is exempt in the partner's hands under Section 10(2A).

02

Section 40(b) Remuneration

Partner salary, interest and commission are deductible only if authorised by the deed and within the limits of Section 40(b). Deed must match the books.

03

Books under Section 44AA

Maintain books of account as prescribed. Depreciation, fixed assets and partner capital accounts must support the computation.

04

Partner ITR-3

Partners typically file ITR-3 for their own income (including remuneration/interest from the firm). Share of profit is exempt under 10(2A).

SECTION 194T

TDS on Payments to Partners (from 1 April 2025)

01

What Attracts TDS

Salary, remuneration, commission, bonus or interest paid to a partner. Aggregate payments to that partner above ₹20,000 in the financial year trigger 10% TDS.

02

When to Deduct

At the time of credit or payment, whichever is earlier. Deposit by the 7th of the following month (Challan 281).

03

Reporting

Report in quarterly Form 26Q. Issue Form 16A to partners. Most firms miss this in the first year — treat drawings correctly.

04

Why It Matters

Non-deduction attracts interest and possible disallowance. Align partner payments with the deed and the TDS calendar.

TAX AUDIT

When Section 44AB Applies

01

Business Turnover

Tax audit if turnover exceeds ₹1 crore (or ₹10 crore if cash receipts/payments are within the 5% limit under current rules).

02

Professional Receipts

Audit if professional receipts exceed ₹50 lakh in the year.

03

Report Due

Form 3CA/3CB with 3CD typically by 30 September. ITR-5 then due by 31 October.

04

Penalty for Non-Audit

0.5% of turnover/receipts, up to ₹1,50,000, for failure to get accounts audited when required.
