OVERVIEW

What is LLP Annual Compliance?

LLP annual compliance is the mandatory set of filings every Limited Liability Partnership in India must complete each financial year under the LLP Act, 2008 — including Form 11 (Annual Return), Form 8 (Statement of Accounts & Solvency), DIR-3 KYC for designated partners and ITR-5 income tax return.

Under Sections 34 and 35, every LLP must file Form 11 with the ROC by 30 May and Form 8 by 30 October. Designated partners with DIN must file DIR-3 KYC by 30 September. ITR-5 is due by 31 July (non-audit) or 31 October (if audit applies). There are no exemptions for dormant or zero-turnover LLPs. Late filing of Form 11 or Form 8 attracts ₹100 per day per form with no upper cap.

CORE MCA FORMS

Form 11 vs Form 8

11

Form 11 — Annual Return

Partner details, capital contribution, registered office, business activities and compliance status. Due by 30 May. Digitally signed by a designated partner; professional certification if turnover/contribution thresholds are crossed.

08

Form 8 — Statement of Accounts & Solvency

Assets, liabilities, income, expenditure and solvency declaration. Due by 30 October. Certified as required and signed by designated partners.

KYC

DIR-3 KYC

Annual KYC for every designated partner holding a DIN. Due by 30 September. Miss it and DIN can be deactivated (₹5,000 to reactivate).

ITR

ITR-5

Income tax return for LLPs. Due 31 July (no audit) or 31 October (with audit). Separate from MCA filings but part of annual compliance.

COMPLIANCE CALENDAR

Key Due Dates (FY Ending 31 March)

30 May — Form 11

File Annual Return on MCA V3. Start early so partner and contribution data is complete.

31 July — ITR-5 (Non-Audit)

File income tax return if tax audit is not applicable.

30 September — DIR-3 KYC & Tax Audit

DIR-3 KYC for all designated partners with DIN. Tax audit report (if applicable) by same date.

30 October — Form 8

File Statement of Accounts & Solvency. Ensure books and solvency statement are ready.

31 October — ITR-5 (Audit)

File ITR-5 if tax audit applies.

31 December — GSTR-9 (If Applicable)

GST annual return if the LLP is GST-registered and required to file GSTR-9.
