OVERVIEW

What is DIR-3 KYC?

DIR-3 KYC is the mandatory annual filing under Rule 12A of the Companies (Appointment and Qualification of Directors) Rules, 2014, by which every Director Identification Number (DIN) holder verifies personal and contact details with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs before 30 September each year.

It applies to every individual allotted a DIN as of 31 March of the relevant year — whether or not they currently serve as a director. Directors use either Form DIR-3 KYC (with DSC, typically first-time or when details change / for reactivation) or Form DIR-3 KYC-WEB (OTP-based, for repeat filers with no change). On-time filing is free (or minimal government fee as applicable); late filing attracts a flat ₹5,000 penalty and automatic DIN deactivation, which prevents signing MCA forms until the DIN is reactivated.

WHO MUST FILE

Every DIN Holder

01

Active Directors

Directors of private limited, public, OPC, Section 8 and other companies.

02

Resigned / Not Associated

Anyone who still holds an active DIN, even if not currently on any board.

03

Disqualified / Dormant

Disqualified directors and directors of dormant companies must still file.

04

New & Foreign Directors

New DIN holders (often within 30 days of allotment) and foreign directors of Indian companies.

The only way to stop the annual requirement is to surrender the DIN through Form DIR-5.

PROCESS

How We File Your DIR-3 KYC

1. Check DIN Status

Verify whether DIN is approved, deactivated or requires full form vs WEB form.

2. Collect Details & Docs

Personal details, Aadhaar, PAN, email, mobile and any change in particulars. Self-attested docs as needed.

3. Prepare Form

DIR-3 KYC (with DSC/certification) or DIR-3 KYC-WEB (OTP). Coordinate Aadhaar/email OTP.

4. Submit on MCA

File on MCA V3 portal, pay fee/penalty if any, obtain SRN and track approval.

5. Confirm Status

Share confirmation. For reactivation, DIN typically moves to Approved within 24–72 working hours after approval.

PENALTY & REACTIVATION

What If You Miss 30 September?

Scenario Consequence
Filed by 30 September No late fee; DIN remains active
Filed after 30 September Flat ₹5,000 late fee; DIN may already be deactivated
DIN deactivated (from 1 Oct) Cannot sign MCA forms, accept new appointments or complete filings that need that DIN
Reactivation File DIR-3 KYC with ₹5,000; after MCA approval, DIN status returns to Approved (often 24–72 hours)
Multiple missed years may require filing for each year with ₹5,000 per year. Company filings (AOC-4, MGT-7, etc.) can be blocked until the director’s DIN is active again.
