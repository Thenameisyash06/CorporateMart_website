03

Fee Retention & Earned Milestones

Treatment of advance payments, milestone completion, and non-remittable outlays

When a payment of fee is made to CorporateMart, the fees paid in advance is retained by CorporateMart. CorporateMart shall acquire the fees upon working on a client's matter. During an engagement, CorporateMart earns fee at different rates and different times depending on the completion of various milestones (e.g. Government Fee Payments, obtaining DIN, Filing of forms, Traveling etc.). Refund cannot be provided for resources and manpower spent on delivering the service which are non-remittable in nature. Further, we cannot refund or credit any money paid to the government entities, such as filing fees or taxes, or to other third parties with a role in processing your order.

Non-Refundable Disbursements Notice:

Statutory payments made to government portals (including MCA ROC filing fees, DIN challans, GST registration stamp duty, PAN/TAN charges, trademark official registry fees, and taxes) and third-party disbursements are strictly non-refundable and cannot be credited or recovered once paid.

06

Change of Service

Transferring funds to an alternative service without forfeiture

If you want to change the service you ordered for a different reason, you must request this change of service within 10 days of purchase. The purchase price of the original service, less the earned/professional fees payable to CorporateMart for availing services(s) plus any fee and/or money paid to the government entities, such as filing fees or taxes, or to other third parties with a role in processing, your order, will be credited to your new/updated service request as has been raised with the CorporateMart.

Benefit of Service Adjustment:

When you opt for a Change of Service within 10 days, no cancellation penalty is levied on the unearned balance, allowing you full flexibility to adapt your legal compliance requirements.

07

Standard Pricing & Government Fee Variations

Base price structure, territorial state rules, and refiling costs

CorporateMart has set a standard pricing policy for various services and client of every category can opt for a service according to their business needs. The basic pricing structure provided on the Website/Emails are base prices. The prices are subject to change with respect to territorial jurisdiction of a state, kind and number of services availed by the client. We reserve the right to change the prices as quoted on our website at any given point of time but this will not affect contracts that have previously come into force. The standard pricing policy is not applicable for an increase in the total fee paid by the client to CorporateMart due to increase in government fee or fee incurred by the client for completion of legal documentation or re-filing of forms with the government due to rejection or resubmission. We are not responsible or liable for any other cost incurred by the client related to the completion of the service. It is possible that prices on the website may be incorrectly quoted; accordingly, we will verify prices as part of our sale procedures so that the correct price will be notified to you before the contract comes into force.

08

Factors outside our Control

Statutory registry bottlenecks, government platform downtime, and partner limitations

We cannot guarantee the results or outcome of your particular procedure. For instance, the government may reject a trademark application for legal reasons beyond the scope of CorporateMart service. In some cases, a government backlog or problems with the government platforms (e.g. MCA website, Income Tax website, FSSAI, start-up India etc.) can lead to long delays before your process is complete. Similarly, CorporateMart does not guarantee the results or outcomes of the services rendered by our Associates or for services provided by the Experts from nearest platform(s), who are not employed by CorporateMart.

Problems like these are beyond our control and are not covered by this guarantee or eligible for refund. Hence, delay in processing of your file by the Government cannot be a reason for refund.

Unforeseen catastrophic occurrences and statutory suspensions

CorporateMart shall not be considered in breach of its Satisfaction Guarantee policy or default under any terms of service, and shall not be liable to the Client for any cessation, interruption, or delay in the performance of its obligations by reason of earthquake, flood, fire, storm, lightning, drought, landslide, hurricane, cyclone, typhoon, tornado, natural disaster, act of God or the public enemy, epidemic, famine or plague, action of a court or public authority, change in law, explosion, war, terrorism, armed conflict, labor strike, lockout, boycott or similar event beyond our reasonable control, whether foreseen or unforeseen (each a "Force Majeure Event").

10

Cancellation Fee Structure

Deductions applicable upon voluntary order cancellation

Since we're incurring costs and dedicating time, manpower, technology resources and effort to your service or document preparation, our guarantee only covers satisfaction issues caused by CorporateMart not changes to your situation or your state of mind. In case you require us to hold the processing of a service, we will hold the fees paid by you until you are ready to commence the service.

Before processing any refund, we reserve the right to make best effort to complete the service. In case, you are not satisfied with the service, a cancellation fee of 20%+ earned/professional fees payable to CorporateMart for availing service(s) + fees paid to government would be applicable. In case of change of service(s), the cancellation fee would not be applicable.

Summary of Cancellation Terms:

  • Voluntary Cancellation: 20% cancellation fee + earned/professional fees incurred + non-refundable government fees paid.
  • Service Hold Option: Fees can be held on credit indefinitely until you are ready to resume processing.
  • Change of Service: Zero cancellation fee applied to the unearned balance.
